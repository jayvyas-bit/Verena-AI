## Core value proposition
AI agents scan a website against six compliance areas — accessibility, terms of
service, privacy, cookies/trackers, commercial email, and marketing claims —
then propose fixes: developer-ready code and draft document revisions. The site
is monitored for changes afterward. `+Human` adds a supervising attorney
(from Invictus Counsel) for anything needing legal judgment rather than pattern
matching.

## Tone and voice
Plain, first-person, reassuring ("Hi, I'm Verena"). Notably **caveat-heavy**:
the copy repeatedly reminds the reader this is beta software, that Verena "can
make mistakes," and that a clean scan does not itself establish legal
compliance. This reads as deliberate trust-building rather than boilerplate
legal cover — it's the site being honest about the limits of an AI agent
doing compliance work, which is exactly the trust gap this audience has (see
`01-audience-emotion.md`).

## What this means for positioning
Verena is not "AI does your compliance" — it's "AI does the first 90%, a human
backs up the last 10%." That's a deliberate, already-present product decision,
not something the redesign needs to invent. The redesign's job is to make that
positioning *read* clearly and calmly in the hero, not to change it.

# Competitors

Confidence: **mixed** — the existence and rough category of each competitor is
solid; most of the specific pricing figures below conflicted across sources
and should be re-verified on each vendor's own site before being used in any
pricing or positioning decision.

## Tier 1 — Point solutions (Verena's actual day-to-day competition for an SMB buyer)

| Tool | Category | Price (as reported — unverified, conflicting) |
|---|---|---|
| Termly | Privacy policy / cookie consent | One source: ~$180/yr unlimited. Another: ~$15/mo entry tier. Has a free plan. |
| CookieYes | Cookie consent | €9–€50/mo per one source; $25/mo per another. Free tier exists. Priced per domain. |
| Osano | Privacy / consent platform | Free tier capped at 5,000 monthly visitors; Plus from ~$199/mo; custom tiers above that. |
| accessiBe | Accessibility overlay | $59–$479/mo by traffic tier per one source; $41/mo entry per another. Custom Enterprise above 100k visits/mo. |
| UserWay | Accessibility overlay | Free basic widget + four paid tiers scaling by monitored pages; enterprise quoted directly. |

**The gap this creates:** a small-business owner today has to buy and manage
2–3 of these separately (one for cookies/privacy, one for accessibility) to
cover what Verena claims to do in a single scan across six compliance areas.
That fragmentation is a real, usable differentiation angle — it doesn't
depend on any of the above pricing numbers being exactly right.

**Unverified claim, flagged, do not repeat as fact:** accessibility overlays
are reported by some sources to carry regulatory/legal scrutiny over whether
they deliver genuine compliance. The research tool itself could not confirm
this against a primary source (e.g. an actual FTC order or court ruling). If
this claim is ever used externally (marketing, competitive positioning), it
needs independent legal verification first — don't cite it from this document
alone.

## Tier 2 — Enterprise GRC platforms (adjacent, not direct competitors today)

Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, OneTrust, Sprinto — built for SOC2/ISO27001
security compliance, sold to security/IT teams at larger companies. Not
competing for Verena's SMB website-compliance buyer today, but:
- They're normalizing "AI agent handles your compliance" as a category,
  which benefits Verena's positioning (see `03-global-startups.md`).
- They're a **watch item for upmarket encroachment** if any of them move
  down into SMB website compliance.

## Pricing comparison (Verena's own numbers, verified in `00-verena-current-state.md`)
Verena's Free → $99 → $299 → $999 ladder is, on its face, cheaper than buying
two or three point tools separately, and far cheaper than an enterprise GRC
contract. This is worth surfacing in copy/design — it's Verena's own
confirmed pricing against a well-established pattern (narrow tools stacking
up in cost), not a claim resting on the shakier competitor price points above.
